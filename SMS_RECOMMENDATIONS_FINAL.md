## 💡 **Professional Best Practices**

### Message Quality Standards:
✅ **DO:**
- Keep under 160 characters when possible
- Use professional language
- Include barangay name
- Provide contact info
- Use proper grammar

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use all caps (except emergencies)
- Send promotional content
- Use abbreviations excessively
- Send during late hours (after 8 PM)

---

### Timing Guidelines:
✅ **Best Times:**
- Business hours: 8 AM - 6 PM
- Weekdays for routine notifications
- Anytime for emergencies

❌ **Avoid:**
- Late night (after 8 PM)
- Early morning (before 7 AM)
- Weekends for non-urgent

---

### Frequency Limits:
✅ **Recommended:**
- Max 1 SMS per resident per day (routine)
- Emergency alerts: No limit
- Weekly digest option for updates

❌ **Avoid:**
- Multiple SMS in short time
- Daily promotional messages
- Unnecessary notifications
